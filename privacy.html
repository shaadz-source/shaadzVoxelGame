Technical Data: We may collect basic system and device information necessary for game compatibility, stability, and server management.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Account Authentication: Your Gmail address is used solely to verify your identity, manage account access, and enforce account bans or terminations where applicable.

Service Updates: We may use your contact information to send critical administrative notices regarding terms updates or account status.

DATA SHARING AND REDISTRIBUTION

No Third-Party Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Gmail address to third parties.

Compliance Safety: We may disclose account details if required by law or if necessary to enforce our Terms of Service (investigating modded clients or prohibited gambling mechanics).

DATA SECURITY We implement standard security measures to protect your account information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
